**What K-17 asks for.** The port takes a town, a ZIP code or a street address. It returns a map point, its precision, a normalized address and the point's time zone (CQ-08, from S13-03), or "not found". It serves B4-77, B4-30, S12-07 and FS rows L02 and L03. The distance itself runs in PostGIS on stored points (Q-25's default), so the storage terms decide the pick.

## 2. K-17 fields, by candidate

- **Fields every candidate fills** (K-17 can freeze these whatever the pick):
  - A point from a street address.
  - A precision. Each provider has its own field, so K-17's precision should be a coarse port enum that each adapter maps to: exact, interpolated, street, ZIP, town.
  - A normalized address: Google and Geocodio `formatted_address`, Mapbox `full_address`, HERE `address.label`, Census its matched address.
  - Not found: the port's answer to an empty result, so no vendor field is needed.
- **Fields all but Census fill:** a point from a ZIP code and a point from a town. Census works only from street addresses, so it can back up street lookups but cannot be the provider.
- **FLAG: time zone (CQ-08).**
  - **Mapbox and Census cannot fill it.**
  - Google fills it only through a second paid call, and its right to store the result is unknown [G5].
  - Geocodio fills it through its append; HERE fills it through `show=tz`.
  - So K-17 should freeze `time_zone` only if the pick is Geocodio or HERE. Otherwise CQ-08's default ("the geo port returns it") fails and needs another source.

## 3. Recommendation for the CTO (Jay approves the cost, Q-25)

1. Pick **Geocodio** for K-17 v1. Of the five, it alone fills every K-17 field, time zone included, and lets Rosie keep the points in PostgreSQL for PostGIS with no time limit [C5]. Google and HERE allow 30 days only, which breaks Q-25's stored-point default; Mapbox has no zone and only conditional storage.
2. For Jay to approve: a new Geocodio pay-as-you-go account (a new account, even a free one, counts as spend: Q-21). It is free up to 2,500 lookups a day, then $1.00 per 1,000 lookups, which is $2.00 per 1,000 points with their zone [C2][C3][C4].
