# Demo images: Unsplash and Pexels

Each demo shop has two search links (shops/<id>.json, `images`). For a handful of shops, open the link and copy a few photo URLs by hand.
To fill all shops at once, use the APIs (both free with a key; check each site's current terms and limits before use):

- **Unsplash**: get an Access Key at https://unsplash.com/developers. Search: `GET https://api.unsplash.com/search/photos?query=<profession>&per_page=5` with header `Authorization: Client-ID <key>`. Use the returned `urls.regular` as is (hotlink, don't re-host), credit the photographer and Unsplash, and call the photo's `links.download_location` when a photo is used.
- **Pexels**: get a key at https://www.pexels.com/api/. Search: `GET https://api.pexels.com/v1/search?query=<profession>&per_page=5` with header `Authorization: <key>`. Use `src.medium` / `src.large`; show a link to Pexels and credit the photographer where you can.
- Demo only: real businesses upload their own photos or bring them from their Google listing [G].
