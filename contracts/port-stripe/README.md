## What it defines

The port in Rosie's own terms (02-contracts K-14, section 5): thirteen calls, the events Stripe sends, and the span every outside call writes. Stripe's paths, headers, field names and codes stay inside the adapters (C-214, C-215); the fixtures are port-level exchanges. Each call is a definition set under `$defs`: `<call>.request`, `<call>.response`, `<call>.errors` and `<call>.exchange` (a fixture); a call that runs from a K-07 effect row also has `<call>.portRequest`. Every field's `description` names its source; "arch NN line N" are the lines of `scratch/text/architecture.txt`.

### Whose account (RULES 3.1; FS rows M01, M04)

`account` is `{"kind": "business", "accountId": ...}` or `{"kind": "rosfield"}`. A business's money lands in its own Stripe account, connected in its own name: the adapter makes every call on it with Stripe's `Stripe-Account` header, so each charge is a direct charge on that account, its balance grows, it pays Stripe's card fee, and it handles its own refunds and disputes (spike rows 1 and 2). Rosie holds no customer money. Rosfield's own account is used only for Rosfield's monthly bill: the bill card, the monthly charge and its one pay link. The schema keeps them apart: purpose `rosfieldMonthlyBill` runs only on Rosfield's account and every other purpose only on a business's (`refuse-monthly-bill-on-business-account.json`).

### Money and cards

- **Money is whole cents with its currency** (CQ-09), as K-07 v1 and K-33 v1 hold it: `{"cents": 4000, "currency": "USD"}`, at least 1 cent, never a decimal (`refuse-decimal-amount.json`). Stripe's amounts are integers in the smallest unit, so the adapter sends the cents as they are and the currency in lower case.
- **Never a hold.** Every charge is captured at once; there is no field for a hold or a later capture (RULES 3.2; D65; `refuse-card-hold.json`). Stripe's `capture_method=manual` is never sent.
- **No card data.** A card is typed into Stripe's own form on Rosie's page, with a client secret, so its number, expiry and CVC never reach Rosie (RULES 3.12, 4.5; spike row 5). The port carries only Stripe's ids and, where a step shows a card, its brand and last four (`cardShown`; A5-07's "Card •••• 4242"; FS row M04). A Stripe id never reads as a card number: 13 or more digits in a row are refused (K-33 v1 `bill_cards_no_card_number`).
- **Client secrets** (`clientSecret`) let whoever holds one finish that one card save or payment. They go only to the page that needs them, and never into a log, a span, an event-log row, a message or K-07's effect row. Fixtures hold placeholders.

### Effects (K-07 v1, K-05 v1.1)

A call that runs from an effect row takes `{effect, amount?, port}`: `effect` is the row's effect id and its one idempotency key, `amount` its amount, and `port` is exactly what the row holds as its `portRequest`. So no fact the effect row holds is stored twice. The effects runner (C-109) builds the request from the row; the adapter sends the key as Stripe's `Idempotency-Key` on every try and puts the effect id on the Stripe object it makes as the metadata key `rosie_effect_id` (spike rows 7 to 9).

| Call              | K-07 kind                                             | K-07 `amount`      | `providerObjectId` on success |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| `saveCard`        | `stripe_save_card`                                    | null               | `setupId`                     |
| `charge`          | `stripe_charge`; `rosfield_monthly_charge` (the bill) | what is due now    | `paymentId`                   |
| `payLink`         | `stripe_pay_link`                                     | what is due        | `paymentId`                   |
| `refund`          | `stripe_refund`                                       | how much goes back | `refundId`                    |
| `disputeEvidence` | `stripe_dispute_evidence`                             | null               | `disputeId`                   |

How the runner records each outcome with K-05 v1.1's `recordEffectResult`:

- **ok:** `charged`, `ready`, `created`, `refunded` and `submitted`, with the `providerObjectId` above. `needsAuthentication` (a charge with the person present whose bank wants them to confirm it) is recorded ok too, with the PaymentIntent and no ledger line: the payment was made and waits on the person's bank check on the page. Whether money was taken comes later as `paymentSucceeded` or `paymentFailed`, linked by the effect id; money words wait for it (RULES 3.14). A charge without the person present never answers `needsAuthentication`: the bank's ask comes back as a decline, `authentication_required` (`refuse-off-session-needs-authentication.json`).
- **declined:** a card decline (HTTP 402 with Stripe's decline code), never retried (RULES 3.11; arch 10 line 542). The step's path follows: "Try another card" on the page, one pay link after the owner's OK (A6-32), or A9-12's "Declined or replaced card".
- **networkError:** `transient` (no answer, a network error, 409, 429 or 5xx), retried on the same key, at most 5 tries in 10 minutes, then that service's outage row (K-07 v1 retry rule 8).
- **`refused`** (any other 4xx: an invalid request, a key repeated with other parameters, an unknown id such as a removed card, an account Rosie may no longer act on): never retried as it is. K-07 v1 has no state for it (its open gap 5; open gap 3 here).

### Pay links (A6-32, A9-05, MC C3, S12-17, S11-68)

A pay link is Rosie's own page (A6-32 texts `rosfield.com/p/f4`; S11-68 `rosfield.com/bill/pay`; C-200). `payLink` makes the PaymentIntent that page takes, with the client secret for Stripe's form, and the effect id on it. One link, never a second (S11-68: "one pay link, with no chasing"). Stripe's own pay links and checkout pages cannot hold Rosie's deadlines (MC C3's 15 minutes; K-33's 14 days; the request's latest answer time), so the deadline is Rosie's: the clock row holds it (`clock.pay`; K-33 v1 `pay_by`), the page refuses after it, and `closePayLink` cancels the payment at it, so nothing can be paid late (spike row 11). If it was paid first, `closePayLink` answers `alreadyPaid` with the time, and the caller weighs it against the deadline; money that lands late is refunded at once (RULES 3.10). While Stripe is down the deadline waits (S12-16).

### Reconciliation's listing (arch 10 line 550; C-112)

`listMoney` gives every charge and refund on one account between two times (03:00 UTC over the last 48 hours), every page of it, each with the effect id it was made under (`rosie_effect_id` on the charge, copied there from its PaymentIntent, and on the refund), its amount, its state and its Stripe id. A declined card leaves a failed charge, listed with its state (`list-48-hours.json`).

- **CQ-15:** on a business's account the listing holds only what Rosie's platform made, since Stripe marks each charge with the Connect application that created it (spike row 10), and each refund of a listed charge. A charge Sam takes in his own Stripe outside Rosie is not listed at all. A listed charge or refund with no effect id (a Rosie charge whose effect id was not stored, or a refund the owner made in Stripe of a Rosie charge) keeps CQ-15's default: no ledger line and no refund by reconciliation; Rosie's team gets it within 1 business day as "anything else" (S12-24; `list-charge-without-effect-id.json`).
- **A booking charged twice** (S12-26): two charges under one effect id, the second with no ledger line and no agreed booking behind it, refunded at once with reason `duplicate` and both told (`list-double-charge.json`, `refund-double-charge.json`). One way it happens: Stripe lets a key go after 24 hours, so an effect run again after a long outage makes a new charge under the same effect id (spike row 7; open gap 4).

## Webhook events

The gateway (C-044) receives Stripe's webhooks on two endpoints, each with its own signing secret (spike rows 13 and 14): `rosfield`, the endpoint for Rosfield's own account, and `businesses`, the endpoint for connected accounts. `verifyWebhook` checks the `Stripe-Signature` header (`t=` and one or more `v1=`; `v0=` is ignored) against `<t>.<raw>` with HMAC-SHA256 under that endpoint's secret, in constant time, within 300 s of receipt; then it parses the body. The gateway answers 200 in under 1 s, drops a known event id with 200, answers 401 to a bad signature, and 503 when its write fails, so Stripe retries (for up to 3 days live; arch 02 line 109).

It stores each event once as a K-07 v1 `providerEvent`: provider `stripe`, `providerEventId` the event id, `eventType` Stripe's type, `occurredAt`, `effectId`, and as `payload` the event's `kind`, `account`, `testMode` and `payload`; `businessId` is the business whose K-23 v1 `stripe_account_id` is the event's account, or for Rosfield's own account the business of the effect it names; `traceId` is the trace opened at receipt.

| `kind`                | Stripe types                                        | Endpoint   | Effect id                  | Steps                               |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| `accountUpdated`      | `account.updated`                                   | businesses | none                       | S1-27, S11-63, S11-65               |
| `accountDisconnected` | `account.application.deauthorized`                  | businesses | none                       | S1-27                               |
| `cardSaved`           | `setup_intent.succeeded`                            | either     | the `saveCard` effect's    | A5-07, S1-29                        |
| `cardSaveFailed`      | `setup_intent.setup_failed`                         | either     | the `saveCard` effect's    | A5-07 ("Try another card")          |
| `cardDetails`         | `payment_method.attached`, `payment_method.updated` | either     | none                       | A5-07's form; FS row M04            |
| `paymentSucceeded`    | `payment_intent.succeeded`                          | either     | the charge's or pay link's | A6-32 "Paid in time", A9-06, S11-68 |
| `paymentFailed`       | `payment_intent.payment_failed`                     | either     | the charge's or pay link's | A6-32, S11-67                       |

- **The card shown** comes in `cardDetails`, joined to `cardSaved` by the saved card's id: Stripe's card-saved event names the card but not its brand or last four, and the gateway makes no Stripe call inside its 1 s answer. The adapter keeps only the brand and last four and drops every other card detail Stripe sends.
- **Test mode:** a production endpoint for connected accounts gets live and test events alike, so each event says `testMode`, and the gateway drops a live event on staging and a test one on production after launch (spike row 14; BP 9.4; DL-020).
- **Order and duplicates:** Stripe sends events in no promised order and may send one more than once, so every handler reads the event's object as it stands and does nothing twice; dedupe is by event id (K-07's key), and Stripe notes it can make two event objects for one change (spike row 13).
- **Event types Rosie does not use** are checked, answered 200 and dropped (`ignored`; `verify-ignored.json`); each endpoint subscribes only to the types above (spike row 13).

## Rosfield's own billing, and K-33 v1

K-33 v1 (contracts/db-schema/platform-billing, frozen) holds the bill, its lines, the pause and the bill card's Stripe ids; this contract holds the calls and what Stripe answers. Nothing is stored twice:

- **The bill card** (S1-29; FS row M04): `saveCard` on Rosfield's account makes Sam's customer there and the card save; `cardSaved` gives the two ids K-33's `bill_cards` stores, `stripe_customer_id` and `stripe_payment_method_id` (`bill-card.json`, `webhook-bill-card-saved.json`, K-33's `seed-bill-card.json`). Their form is K-14's: Stripe's opaque ids, never a card number (K-33's open gap on id forms is answered: no form check beyond that). The card's brand and last four, which K-33 stores nowhere, come from `cardDetails` (K-33's open gap on the card's display).
- **The monthly charge** (S11-67): `charge` with purpose `rosfieldMonthlyBill` on the bill card, without Sam present, run as K-07's `rosfield_monthly_charge` effect whose amount is the bill's total (`monthly-charge.json`, K-33's `seed-monthly-bill-charged.json`). A decline is never retried.
- **The one pay link** (S11-68): `payLink` on Rosfield's account, run as `stripe_pay_link`; the link's Stripe id is its `paymentId`, the effect's `providerObjectId` (`bill-pay-link.json`, K-33's `seed-bill-unpaid-pause.json`). Paid: `paymentSucceeded`, which the runtime side records as K-33's `paid_at`.
- **A replaced bill card:** K-33 marks the old row `removed_at`; `removeCard` removes the old card on Stripe once no business's live row names it.
- K-07's and K-33's v1 fixtures hold placeholders that predate this contract: K-07's `portRequest` (`{"purpose": "deposit", "savedCard": ...}`, `{"refundOf": ...}`) and K-33's `port_request` (`{"monthlyBillId": ...}`), and Stripe ids named after the charge (`stripe-charge-demo-0001`, `stripe-charge-demo-yoga-studio-2026-10`) where this contract stores the PaymentIntent. Both contracts take any object and any id there, so nothing breaks; the seeding and the effects runner write K-14's shapes (open gap 7).

## The double (02-contracts K-14; C-214, C-215)

The Stripe double (test code only) answers every call with any outcome this contract defines, as the test chooses, in Stripe's test-card terms; it records every request; it signs webhooks (`doubleWebhook`) and plants charges for reconciliation (`doublePlant`). - **Its reconciliation hooks:** `listMoney` on the double lists the charges and refunds it made between two times, each with its effect id; `doublePlant` plants a charge with no effect id (`double-plant-charge-without-effect-id.json`) or a second charge under one effect id (`double-plant-second-charge.json`).

The double signs for the endpoint whose account the event names: `businesses` for a business's account, `rosfield` for Rosfield's.

## Defaults used (T-023)

1. **The account type:** Standard, through controller properties (the full Dashboard; the account pays Stripe's fees; Stripe liable for losses; Stripe collects requirements), with direct charges, so every charge is the business's own in its own name (RULES 3.1; S1-27; spike rows 1 and 2). Accounts v1 with controller properties; Stripe's Accounts v2 would change only the adapter.
2. **Q-22:** no application fee on any charge: Rosfield's $2 and 1% go on the monthly bill (K-33), so a charge has no fee field. If Jay picks "out of one payment", a fee is an optional field in a minor version (spike row 2).
3. **Secret names:** `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (Rosfield's account) and `STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (connected accounts), until the CTO and the release engineer name them (02-contracts section 2 item 13). Rosie's Connect application id, which the listing compares `Charge.application` with, is configuration, not a secret.
4. **Snapshot events on two endpoints:** the gateway parses the object the event carries and makes no Stripe call inside its 1 s answer, though Stripe recommends thin events for new integrations (spike row 13).
5. **A pay link is a PaymentIntent on Rosie's own pay page**, closed by `closePayLink` at Rosie's deadline (A6-32's and S11-68's links are rosfield.com pages; spike row 11).
6. **Event types Rosie does not use** are checked, answered 200 and dropped (`ignored`).
7. **`providerObjectId`** is the object the call made: the SetupIntent, the PaymentIntent (for a charge and a pay link alike), the refund, the dispute.
8. **The effect id rides as metadata `rosie_effect_id`** on the SetupIntent, the PaymentIntent and the refund (spike rows 8 and 9).
9. **`customerPresent`:** a charge says whether the person is on the page; only then can it answer `needsAuthentication`, which the runner records as ok, with no ledger line until `paymentSucceeded`.
10. **The failure kinds:** `declined` is Stripe's card error (402); `transient` is no answer, 409, 429 or 5xx; `refused` is any other 4xx, never retried as it is.
11. **CQ-15:** the listing leaves out charges Rosie's platform did not make (`Charge.application`); a listed item with no effect id takes CQ-15's default.
12. **A Stripe customer holds no name or contact detail from Rosie**: the business's Stripe shows its customers by id (open gap 6).
13. **The webhook window is 300 s**, arch 02 line 109's for Linq and Stripe's libraries' default.
14. **Each try's timeout stays under 60 s** (K-05 v1.1 open gap 12's proposal).
15. **Removing a saved card and closing a pay link are port calls** run by the turn or job that decides them, retried as other jobs are (arch 10 line 540), until K-07 has effect kinds for them (open gap 2).
16. **Dispute evidence only on a business's account**, as a private file (K-13) with the visit's date, description and the customer's name; no person's words in the request.

## Open gaps

1. **Q-55** (Stripe test keys, Connect settings and a staging webhook; J-12): the double meanwhile. 4. **Stripe's key life against K-07's outage path:** Stripe returns a saved 500 for the same key for 24 hours, and lets a key go after 24 hours, after which the same key makes a new charge. So an effect whose 5 tries all got Stripe's saved 500 takes its outage row, and an effect run again after an outage longer than 24 hours can charge twice; reconciliation catches a second charge under one effect id and refunds it at once (S12-26). Proposed for C-109: before a run after an outage, look for a PaymentIntent already carrying the effect id (the listing, or Stripe's search, under 1 minute behind) and record it rather than charge again. New question for `QUESTIONS.md`.
5. **FS row M01's name check:** the account is made with the business's registered name, but a later change in the owner's own Stripe is not seen: `accountState` does not report Stripe's business name. Proposed: an optional field in a minor version, with what a mismatch does (no source says).
