# tool-api (K-09): the Tool API

**Status: v1 DRAFT, not frozen.** Drafted by T-009: the turn face and the shared core only. There is no `frozen.json`, no fixtures and no consumer review yet. T-018 adds journeys 1 and 2, the fixtures and the reviews, and freezes v1 (02-contracts K-09, "Freeze": F5). Until then any call here may change.

**Owner:** the architect. **Schema:** `tool-api.v1.schema.json` (JSON Schema 2020-12, `$id` `https://rosie.invalid/contracts/tool-api/tool-api.v1.schema.json`). There is no root payload: each call's request, response and errors are `$defs` named `<call>.request`, `<call>.response` and `<call>.errors` (02-contracts section 2 item 2), and `$defs/callName` lists the calls. **Checked by:** `npm run contracts:check` (the schema compiles in Ajv's strict 2020-12 mode and every `$ref` resolves). **Contract test:** `tests/contract/tool-api.v1.test.ts` (QA, with the freeze, T-018). **Mock:** the Tool API mock (C-186, T-510) is generated from the fixtures after the freeze (QUESTIONS.md M2-qa-frontend.1).

## What it defines

The calls that own every read and write (arch 02 line 139). The same calls serve texts, secure pages and outside AIs under the same limits; free text from pages and AIs gets the safety read first (arch 02 line 129); row-level security scopes every call by the caller's business (arch 10 line 546; K-04 v1 rule 4). Every request carries a `caller` (`$defs/caller`): who calls (a turn, a page, an agent, the console, a job, a clock firing, or the handle actor writing on return), the business it runs under, the person it acts for, the key a repeat is known by, and the trace it joins (K-08 v1).

Every field's `description` names its source (02-contracts section 2 item 4). "arch NN line N" are the lines of `scratch/text/architecture.txt`, as `ops/plan/02-contracts.md` cites them. Frozen contracts are reused by relative `$ref` where a call carries their shapes: K-01 v1 (`stepId`), K-02 v1 (`context`, `text`, `contextAndDecide`, `plannerOutput`, `safetyResult`, `safetyOutcome`), K-03 v1 (`composeInput`, `rowPlan`, `finalParts`, `checkResult`), K-05 v1 (`turn`, `commitResult`, `rowRef`, `sendClass`, `phoneNumber` and its errors), K-07 v1 (`effectKind`, `rowState`), K-08 v1 (`traceId`, `limitResult`) and the shared formats. The table parts K-29, K-34, K-36 and K-37 are table lists, so the calls name their columns and closed lists in words and enums, never by `$ref`.

**CQ-25 (settled):** decide reaches the model in two calls a read, made by the AI gateway (T-361). The turn face passes K-02 v1's context to decide, and takes K-02 v1's decide result back, unchanged; the wire form is C-115's and C-121's, not the Tool API's.

Every call can also answer `transient` (K-05 v1's: a serialization failure, a deadlock or a lost connection): nothing was written, and the same request may be sent again.

### In a turn or alone

A turn commits once (arch 02 line 121; arch 10 line 508). So a shared-core call made in a turn (a STOP, a safety outcome, a door, an owner's answer to a hand-off) rides inside `gateAndCommit`: its request in `request.core` (`$defs/coreAction`, with `caller.kind` turn) and its response or error in `response.core` (`$defs/coreResult`). A refused core action stops only itself. A call made alone (by a page, an agent, the console, a job or a clock firing with no handle) commits once under `caller.requestId`. Each call's table row says which ways it runs.

### Limits 1 to 6 at the gate (`gateAndCommit`)

Each part of a committed turn comes back as `$defs/partResult`: `committed`; `stopped`, with `stoppedBy`, the failed limit and its outcome; `dependsOnStopped`; `held` by a safety hold; `asked` (decide ended it in one question); or `noAction`. `limits` lists each limit's result as K-08 v1's `limitResult`, which the gate span logs. Each outcome's kind is one of `ask`, `refuse` and `wait` (`$defs/gateOutcome`), and each limit's actions are an enum in `limit1Outcome` to `limit6Outcome`, one kind per action:

| Limit         | Action               | Kind   | What happens (arch 06)                                                                                  |
| ------------- | -------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Allowed     | `proofCodeSent`      | wait   | Unproven sender: a code to the contact on file, in words that never confirm a booking exists (line 299) |
| 1 Allowed     | `askedDecider`       | ask    | Wrong person: a question in the decider's own chat (line 299)                                           |
| 2 Owner rules | `saidWhatRuleAllows` | refuse | Rosie says what the rule allows; nothing is saved (line 301)                                            |
| 3 Exact yes   | `freshReadBack`      | ask    | A fresh read-back, once (line 303)                                                                      |
| 3 Exact yes   | `safeDefault`        | refuse | Then the step's safe default, and the owner is told (line 303)                                          |
| 4 Versions    | `freshYes`           | ask    | Changed but still possible: today's facts and a fresh yes (line 305)                                    |
| 4 Versions    | `saidWhatChanged`    | refuse | No longer possible: say what changed and offer the current choices (line 305)                           |
| 4 Versions    | `toPerson`           | wait   | A second conflict on one question: a person (line 305)                                                  |
| 5 Reserved    | `nextOpenTimes`      | ask    | The next open times (line 307)                                                                          |
| 5 Reserved    | `nothingBooked`      | refuse | No pick in 10 minutes on the page, or 2 of their text hours: nothing booked (line 307; A6-11)           |
| 6 Values      | `oneQuestion`        | ask    | After decide's one Sonnet re-read, one question (line 309)                                              |
| 6 Values      | `nothingChanged`     | refuse | Then nothing changes (line 309)                                                                         |

Limit 4 reloads once before it fails: nothing shown changed means the choice commits (line 305). A K-05 v1 `versionChanged` or `reservationLost` from the commit is turned into limit 4's or limit 5's outcome for the parts it touches, never returned as an error. Limits 7 (claim check) and 8 (at send) are `finalizeMessage`'s and the dispatcher's.

## Defaults used (T-009)

1. **Q-03:** a chat link is shown only once the mobile is a Linq contact; until contacts/add has worked `issueChatLink` answers `waitingForContact` (the effect row, CQ-04), and if it failed `contactAddFailed`, with the booking page, email or the person's own AI as the other ways.
2. **Q-18:** Rosie's team is Jay and the CTO until launch: they get pages, decide review items and follow up harm reports (`notTeam` otherwise); on staging only roster test phones and the test console (BP 9.4).
3. **New, `closestEntries`' budget:** no source gives one. Proposed: at most 200 ms, the "Search, one indexed query" budget (arch 04 line 217, DD), since meaning search is one indexed query.
4. **Q-40 and Q-42:** an any-time row with no handler step is not offered as a choice (K-02 v1 default 4); the meaning-match index is named by its spike, and while it cannot be read `closestEntries` answers `indexUnavailable` and the turn goes on to the planner (arch 03 line 189).
5. **New, a text's safety outcome:** each text's outcome, No included, is a `recordSafetyOutcome` core action of the turn its text joins, since K-34 v1 writes one outcome row per text with that turn's id. With danger, the actor closes the batch at once, so the checked line goes first (arch 02 line 111: "goes first, at any hour, past any opt-out") as an outbox row of send class safety within 5 s (NQ-RT-9). How the batch closes is the runtime's (C-047, C-048) to confirm.
6. **New, stop and start replies:** while PostgreSQL is up, the STOP, START and HELP replies are outbox rows of send class quick_reply, a reply in the same turn (Q-15); only while it is down does the actor send them straight through Linq, written on return through `sentByActor` (NQ-RT-9's rule for the safety line, applied to the stop lines; arch 11 line 566).
7. **CQ-05:** text hours, and a work phone's "next text hour" (RULES 7.7), are read in the business's zone for owners and staff, and in the visit's zone, else the business's, for customers.
8. **Q-45:** nothing covers an outage of Cloudflare itself, so `bookingPausedError` exists only while the Workers run.
9. **New, the kind of each limit's outcome:** arch 06 lines 299 to 309 give each limit's actions; 02-contracts K-09 gives the three kinds (ask, refuse, wait); the pairing in the table above is this draft's.
10. **New, one commit per turn:** the planner's verbs and every shared-core call made in a turn ride inside `gateAndCommit` (arch 02 line 121), so `plannerVerbs` has no call of its own; a call made alone commits once under `caller.requestId`.
11. **New, the fallback is code's:** `finalizeMessage` fills the step's fallback text itself after a failed rewrite (arch 02 line 123: no model in it), so one call returns at most two check results and ends in send, sendAndLog, rewrite or passedOn.
12. **New, proof codes:** the code goes to the email on file, or the person texts it from the mobile it proves, since Rosie never texts first (MC C9); on the email route the code never comes back to the caller.
13. **NQ-RT-4:** a clock's firing is a turn on the handle's actor (or a sweep job) whose only input is the clock row; it calls `gateAndCommit` with `input.kind` clock and no decide reads, and commits through K-05 v1 `fireClock`.

## Open gaps

1. **K-05 v1.1 (T-071, in flight):** effect rows written at the commit (`gateAndCommit.response.effects` stays empty until it freezes), the waiting rows' moves to superseded or withdrawn (K-07 v1), `dispatcherGivesUp` behind `sendsFailing`, and provider events. Named as K-05 v1.1's, never `$ref`'d here.
2. **A commit with no turn:** a call made alone by a page, an agent, the console or a job writes rows, but K-05 v1 commits only a turn (`commitTurn`) or a clock firing (`fireClock`). It needs a K-05 call that commits under a job or request id (K-05 v1.1's or a later minor version). New question for QUESTIONS.md.
3. **K-05 v1.2 (T-072):** the guard reads a handle's targets through K-05 v1.2's state reads; `buildFrontier` takes them as given (`guardTargets`).
4. **K-08 v1's event kinds** have none for safety outcomes and holds, harm reports, hand-offs, team pages, review items, gap cases, chat links or proof codes. Each call here writes event-log rows, so K-08 needs a minor version adding them before T-018's freeze.
5. **Parts not yet frozen:** K-22 (where proof codes and sign-ins are kept, and whom the owner names for a hand-off or a page), K-27 (the calendar link a dropped link names) and K-16 (the clash check's fresh read). The calls carry their ids as opaque strings until they freeze.
6. **K-29 v1 chat links have two purposes,** booking and staff_invite. 02-contracts K-09 also names "a person's" link; it needs a K-29 minor version, so v1 issues the two.
7. **The planner's protecting change** (arch 06 line 291): K-02 v1's five planner verbs hold none (K-02 open gaps), so a protecting change reaches the gate through decide's `enter` on its any-time row.
8. **CQ-32:** how a paged person's reply reaches a page. Here, `acknowledgePage` as a core action of that person's turn, or a console tap.
9. **Fixed lines:** the outage lines, the stop, start and HELP replies and the safety lines are K-01 fixed lines by id; their words wait on Q-01 and Jay's review.
10. **Rosie's number and the link host** in `issueChatLink` are config values (Q-51).
11. **`bookingPausedError.retryAfterSeconds`:** no source gives it; the runtime sets it.
12. **Time budgets:** only `buildFrontier` (150 ms) and `gateAndCommit` (300 ms) have one in the sources; the rest are open.
13. **Defaults 3, 5, 6 and 9 to 12** are new and go to QUESTIONS.md with this draft.
