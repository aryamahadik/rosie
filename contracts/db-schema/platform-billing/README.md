## What it defines

Rosfield's own billing of each business, following 02-contracts section 7 row K-33: the monthly bill with its lines for last month's $2s and 1%s, billed on the 1st or the next weekday and charged 2 days later, a line that can be questioned; the bill card saved at sign-up (S1-29; FS row M04); the bill's one pay link; and the pause on new Rosie bookings while it stays unpaid (S11-66 to S11-69). Arch 09 gives the domain 2 tables and no names (line 470); this part holds 3 (open gaps). Every table and column names its source in `platform-billing.v1.tables.json`.

| Table                | What it holds                                                                                                                                                                                             | Source                                                 |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| `bill_cards`         | The card for Rosfield's monthly bill, per business: only Stripe's opaque customer and payment-method ids, who saved it and when; one live card per business, and one card may pay every company's bill.   | FS row M04; S1-29; S11-67; RULES 4.5                   |
| `monthly_bills`      | One bill per company (business) and month: its bill day and charge day, total, card, state, the charge and pay link as K-07 effect rows, the pay-by day, when it was paid, and the pause on new bookings. | S11-66 to S11-69; S1-29; FS line 404; arch 10 line 540 |
| `monthly_bill_lines` | Each line: a $2 for a booking or 1% of a plan charge, the K-32 fee it bills, the booking and the person whose cap it counts toward, its amount, and the owner's question with Rosie's team's answer.      | S11-66; S1-25; A9-21; FS lines 394, 733                |

### Who owns what

- **Here (K-33):** the bill card as Stripe's opaque ids; the bill, its days, total, state, pay-by day, paid and paused times; the lines as the bill shows them, with the question and answer.
- **K-14 (Stripe port, not frozen yet, F9):** the card itself and everything about it on Stripe, the form of the customer and payment-method ids, the charge and pay-link requests and results. No card number, expiry or card detail is ever stored here (RULES 4.5; BP rule 7); `bill_cards_no_card_number` refuses a run of 13 or more digits in either id.
- **K-07 v1 (outbox and effect rows, frozen, #77):** the effect rows the charge (`rosfield_monthly_charge`) and the pay link (`stripe_pay_link`) run through, with their idempotency key, tries, state, Stripe's object id and error, and daily reconciliation, including its refund of a charge with no agreed booking. A bill names its two effects by `charge_effect_id` and `pay_link_effect_id` (foreign keys to `effects.id`) and repeats none of their columns. **K-05 v1.1 (T-071):** the calls that write and claim those effect rows and record their results.
- **K-32 (money, frozen later, F16):** the counting of each fee, `rosfield_2` or `rosfield_1pct` (FS fee row: counted at, counted by check-in code or DONE, person given the work, month, status), the ledger, and whether a $2 came out of a payment through Rosie or goes on the bill (Q-22). A line names its fee by `fee_id`; K-32 adds that foreign key, as K-24 does for K-23's `kind_id`.
- **K-23 (business setup, frozen, #78):** the company and its business (`companies.business_id`), and the owner's own PAUSE (`new_booking_pauses`), which is not this part's pause. **K-04 core:** businesses, persons, bookings, and the minute sweep's clock row for monthly billing (a `job` clock with no handle, arch 10 line 540).

### The bill card (S1-29; FS row M04)

- One row per business and card; `bill_cards_one_live` allows one live card (`removed_at` null) per business. A new card is a new row, and the old one gets `removed_at`. "One card can pay every company's bill" (S1-29): the owner's one card sits on a row of each business with the same Stripe ids, and `bill_cards_payment_method_idx` finds every business a card pays.
- Saved at S1-29 ("add it now, or before the first bill"), only where the $2 applies (S1-25's `pf:no2` variant asks no card). `saved_by_person_id` is the owner, never a helper (S1-77).

### The bill (S11-66, S11-67)

- **One bill per company and month** (`monthly_bills_business_month_key`), for last month (`month`, its first day).
- **Sent on the 1st or the next weekday** (`monthly_bills_bill_date_rule`: the 1st, or Monday the 3rd when the 1st is a Saturday, Monday the 2nd when it is a Sunday), **charged 2 days later** (`monthly_bills_charge_two_days_later`). Nov 1 2026 is a Sunday, so October's bills come Mon Nov 2 and are charged Wed Nov 4 (S11-66). No public holiday moves either day: the sources name none.
- **The total** (`total_cents`, USD) is the standing lines with each person's $2s capped at $49 for the month and the 1%s uncapped (S1-25, S11-66, A9-21). Code sums it from the lines and sums it again when a line comes off before the charge; the cap spans rows, so no constraint holds it.
- **The states** (`monthly_bills_state_matches`): `sent` → `charged` (the card went through), or `sent` → `pay_link` (declined) → `paid` or `paused` → `paid`; with no bill card the bill is written straight in `pay_link`.
- **The charge** goes on the bill card of the bill's own business (`monthly_bills_card_fk`), once: one `charge_effect_id`, unique, and a decline is never retried (S11-67; arch 10 line 542). Charged and declined are never both (`monthly_bills_charge_on_card`).

### The one pay link (S11-67, S11-68)

- Exactly when there is no bill card, or the card was declined (`monthly_bills_pay_link_when_due`), the bill has one pay link: `pay_link_effect_id`, `pay_link_sent_at` and `pay_by` together. One column, so never a second link: "Sam gets one pay link, with no chasing".
- **14 days to pay** (`monthly_bills_pay_by_fourteen_days`): `pay_by` is `bill_date + 14` with no card, `charge_on + 14` after a decline (Wed Nov 4, pay by Wed Nov 18).
- **Paid** (`paid_at`) only by the link (`monthly_bills_paid_by_link`); Stripe's payment arrives as a K-07 provider event, and the runtime side records it.

### The pause on new bookings (S11-69)

- "Still unpaid in time": the job finds pay links past due (`monthly_bills_pay_by_idx`) and sets `state` `paused` with `paused_at`, never sooner than 14 days after the link went (`monthly_bills_paused_after_pay_by`; S11-67's safe default: "new bookings go on until the bill's pay link is past due"). Code pauses after the whole `pay_by` day has passed in the business's zone.
- **The read:** a business's new bookings through Rosie are paused while one of its bills is in state `paused` (`monthly_bills_paused_idx`). Then AI assistants and the booking page hear "not taking new bookings right now", as after a PAUSE, and customers are not told why; bookings already made, walk-ins and the owner's own bookings carry on; a call to the business's line gets the call notice and a message to the owner (S11-69; S13-56). Paying the link ends it (`paid_at` after `paused_at`: `monthly_bills_paid_ends_pause`).
- Not K-23's `new_booking_pauses` (the owner's PAUSE and RESUME, S11-15) and not core's `pauses` (a person, phone or address held, A3-48). A booking path checks all three.

### The lines (S11-66; S1-25; A9-21)

- `rosfield_2`: exactly 200 cents, with its booking and the person whose cap it counts toward: who gave the work (a class's teacher, a table's server, never whoever checked it in), or the owner when nobody gave it (`monthly_bill_lines_two_dollars`). `rosfield_1pct`: 1% of `plan_charge_cents`, rounded to a whole cent either way, no cap person (`monthly_bill_lines_one_percent`). USD only.
- One line per K-32 fee (`monthly_bill_lines_fee_key`), so a fee is never billed twice. Which fees a month's bill takes (walk-ins, the business's own bookings, cancelled or missed visits and no-$2 work are free; a fee that came out of a payment through Rosie never goes on the bill) is K-32's counting, read by the monthly job.
- **A line can be questioned** in the portal before the charge (`questioned_at`, `questioned_by_person_id`, optional `question`). A person on Rosie's team answers (`answered_at`, `answered_by_person_id`, `outcome` `stands` or `removed`, together and after the question: `monthly_bill_lines_answer_together`); a removed line comes off the total. The team console lists open questions by `monthly_bill_lines_open_question_idx`.

## Database rules beyond the table list

The table list's meta-schema has no place for these; the migrations must produce them too, and T-110 checks them.

1. **Same schema as core:** the three tables live beside K-04's tables and K-07's `effects`, and their foreign keys name them by key (`businesses.id`, `persons.id`, `bookings.id`, `effects.id`), so the references carry no schema prefix. The migration therefore runs after K-05 v1.1's, which migrates `effects`. No table here refers to K-23's tables; K-32 later adds the foreign key from `monthly_bill_lines.fee_id` to its fee table.
2. **Who may do what, by privilege** (K-04 v1 rule 4's two sides), each table scoped by `business_id` for the Tool API:
   - `bill_cards`: the Tool API may select and insert, and update only `removed_at` and `version` (S1-29's screen through K-21, and a card replaced). Stripe's ids are never changed in place: a new card is a new row.
   - `monthly_bills`: the Tool API may only select (the owner's Money page, the pause read). The runtime side writes every bill: the monthly billing job (the minute sweep's clock row), the charge and pay-link results through K-05 v1.1, the payment's provider event, the pause.
   - `monthly_bill_lines`: the Tool API may select, and update only `questioned_at`, `questioned_by_person_id`, `question` and `version` (the owner questions a line). The lines are written, and the answer is given, on the runtime side, where Rosie's team works until K-21 names its role (K-37 v1 rule 2). An owner's attempt to answer is refused with SQLSTATE 42501.
3. **Versions:** every table is updated (a card removed, a bill charged, declined, paid or paused, a line questioned or answered), so each has `version integer not null default 1`, raised by every update and compared and set by a commit (arch 01 line 85; K-04 v1 rule 2).
4. **Rules across rows and tables, kept by code** (not constraints): a bill's total is its standing lines with each person's $2s capped at 4900 cents; the charge effect's amount is the total when it is written; a bill takes the business's live card when it is made; a line's booking and fee belong to the bill's business and month; a question comes before `charge_on`; whoever answers is on Rosie's team (Q-18); a card on several businesses belongs to one owner; the charge is written on `charge_on` and the pause after the whole `pay_by` day, in the business's zone.
5. **Ids** are `text`, opaque strings code makes (CQ-09). **Money** is whole cents (`bigint`) with a currency code (CQ-09), USD only, since Rosfield's fee terms are stated in US dollars ($2, $49).

## Open gaps

- **Arch 09's count:** 2 tables for "Platform billing"; this part holds 3 (the card, the bill, its lines). Arch 09 gives no names, so the count cannot be matched table for table (as K-29, K-36 and K-37 found for theirs).
- **The 25th $2 and the $49 cap:** $49 is not a whole number of $2s. Whether the 25th $2 of a month counts $1 or $2 before the cap is reached is not in the sources; v1 keeps every line at $2 and caps the total at $49 per person, so the 25th counts $1 in effect. Proposed for QUESTIONS.md with Q-22.
- **Rounding the 1%** of a plan charge to a whole cent (up, down or to nearest): not in the sources; v1 allows either whole cent next to the exact 1%.
- **A month with nothing to bill:** whether a company with no counted $2 or 1% gets a $0 bill is not in the sources; v1 allows `total_cents` 0 and code decides.
- **A line questioned and not answered by the charge day:** whether the charge waits, or goes ahead and the answer refunds the line (through K-07's refund effect), is not in the sources; v1 records the question and the answer and leaves the charge to code.
- **The team's queue for a questioned line:** K-37 v1's `review_items` has no kind for it, so the team console reads `monthly_bill_lines_open_question_idx`; a `bill_line` review kind would be a K-37 v1.1 change.
- **A card added after a bill went with its pay link:** whether the bill is then charged on it is not in the sources; v1 keeps the pay link.
- **Holidays:** "the next weekday" (S11-66) names no holidays, so v1 moves the bill day for weekends only.
- **The card's display** (brand and last four on the owner's pages, FS M04): Stripe's, read through K-14; nothing is stored here.
- **K-14's id forms** for the Stripe customer and payment method: text with no form until K-14 freezes (F9); a form check would be a v1.1 constraint.
- **K-07 and K-08 vocabularies:** the charge and pay-link effect states and the event kinds for a bill, a question, an answer and a pause are K-07's and K-08's.
- **CQ-09's id forms:** ids are opaque text until the CTO rules.
