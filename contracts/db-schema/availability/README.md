### Who owns what

One fact lives in one place. Where another part holds a field, this part refers to it and never repeats it.

- **K-04 core** owns `businesses` (`time_zone`), `persons`, `resources` (worker, room, vehicle, class session), `class_seats` (`capacity` and `taken`: the counter that binds, CHECK `taken <= capacity`), `reservations` (bookings and saved times, with EXCLUDE per worker, room or vehicle and per worker across businesses), `sealing_keys`, `open_questions` and `clock_rows`. A class session is a K-04 resource row; this part adds its time, teacher, room and minimum and never redefines the resource or its counter. A worker's hours and time off name the worker's K-04 resource, as K-25's price rules do.
- **K-22 people and access** owns the team: `staff` (a team member at one business; FS line 723's `calendar_link` is `calendar_links` here), `team_powers` (who may answer a day off for the owner), and `on_call` (who takes jobs sent now, which is not open hours). Its seeds hold Sam's and Riley's sealing keys, which seal their links' tokens. Nothing here refers to a K-22 table: a link's person is K-04's `persons`, since an owner has no `staff` row.
- **K-23 business setup** owns `locations` (the owner of opening hours, H01) and `places_or_things` (whose hours and closed days are rows here: K-23 v1 README). Its `flow_defaults` holds the free-cancel time; nothing here repeats it.
- **K-25 services and prices** owns `services`: a class and a session name the service a seat sells by `(service_id, business_id)`, K-25's `services_id_business_key` (`classes_service_fk`, `class_sessions_service_fk`), so a seat sells a service of its own business. K-24 owns a service's kind of work, which the seeds' services name (`yoga`).
- **K-07 outbox and effects** owns Rosie's own calendar events: a `calendar_write`, `calendar_update` or `calendar_delete` effect per visit and booked person, with the provider's event id (`effects.provider_object_id`), and every calendar push stored once in `provider_events`. Rosie's own events never come back in as busy (arch 05 line 267), so no busy block is written for one.
- **K-16 calendar port** (v1, frozen, #117) owns OAuth, watching and renewing, reading changes, the fresh free/busy read and the events out: the inner form of the sealed tokens, the subscription mechanics and the event ids are its own. A link here holds only what the database needs; K-16's `calendarLinkId` is `calendar_links.id`, a person's ("a link belongs to a person, never to one business").
- **K-02 v1.1** (photo reading, frozen with this part, F14) reads a class timetable into `classes`' day, start and end, teacher and room, never the seats or the minimum, which the owner is asked for (S1-83); with no end on the photo, code takes the end from the service's length (K-25 `services.length`). It reads a schedule into busy blocks' times, never what the job is (S1-24).
- **K-30 bookings** copies a booking line's service and price; the seats of a session booked or saved are K-04 reservations of the session's resource.

### Hours (H01; S1-21; S1-81)

- One row per open span on one day of the week, as local times read in the zone the row names (`time_zone`, the business's, copied when written). A span past midnight is two rows, one on each day; `24:00` closes at midnight. Two spans of one owner on one day never overlap (`weekly_hours_no_overlap`), so the hours read back once.
- The owner is a location (its opening hours, H01), a resource (a worker's own hours, at one of the business's locations or any; a room's or vehicle's) or a space or thing (S1-81). A class session's time is its own row, never an hours row.
- H01 comes from the Google listing as prefill, and the owner confirms each span (FS section 10); no column records that, the event log does.
- An open time for a worker is inside the hours of the location they work at then and, when they have spans of their own, inside those too; with none, the location's hours (default 4).

### Time off (H02; S11-13; S11-43 to S11-45, S11-89)

- **A team member's day off** (S11-43, `OFF Mon Nov 2`): a row of kind `off`, status `asked`, by the team member, under the business they name (OFF asks "Which business?" when both have work that day). New bookings with them in the window pause while it is asked (S11-43, S11-78). The owner's 1 OK makes it `ok`, 2 Say no `declined` (the day opens again: S11-45); no answer by 8:00am the day before, in the row's zone, makes it `lapsed` by the clock: the day stays as booked and the pause lifts (S11-44, S11-89). Who may answer is the owner, or an admin with that power (K-22 `team_powers`).
- **The owner's BLOCK** (S11-13): a row of kind `blocked` for the whole business (or a location or person), `ok` on the read-back's yes. Visits already booked inside it stay as they are until the owner picks what happens to them ("Block; offer new times" or "Block around it"); those are K-04 reservations and K-30 changes (S11-14, S11-106).
- **Closed**: a location, room, vehicle, space or thing closed for a day or a holiday (S1-81: "can be closed for a day, like a person's time off"; S1-83: "a closed room shows on the Team page"). A person is off, never closed (`time_off_kind_fits_owner`).
- **A person with no linked calendar** blocks their own time by text (Q-10): a `blocked` row on their resource.
- Only `asked` and `ok` rows take times out of offer. No reason is kept: a reason can be a health detail (RULES 4.5).

### Classes and sessions (S1-83; A4-33)

- A class meets each week: its service, day, start and end (local times with their zone), teacher, room, place (a shop, or a meeting spot with where it ends), the seats Rosie can book and the fewest it needs to run. The seats and minimum are asked of the owner, never read from a photo (K-02 v1.1).
- Each session is a K-04 `resources` row of kind `class_session`, its `class_seats` counter (capacity from the class's seats) and its `class_sessions` row, written in one commit (default 10). A one-off session, such as the Handstand workshop, has no class. Customers book a seat, never a person (A4-33); the seats booked and saved are K-04 reservations of the session's resource.
- At the cutoff, Rosie counts the bookings (`counted_at`, `too_few`). Too few: the owner decides to run it or cancel, and with no answer it runs; a cancelled session is `cancelled` with who and when, its resource stops being bookable in the same commit, and anything paid goes back to everyone (S1-83; K-30, K-32).
- If the teacher is out, the owner puts someone in (the session's own teacher); nothing moves for the customer (A4-33). A room or a teacher is never in two scheduled sessions at once (`class_sessions_one_per_room`, `class_sessions_one_per_teacher`). A scheduled session is busy time for its teacher and room, which the time finder takes out (code: a session is not a K-04 reservation of them).

### Calendar links and busy blocks (S1-24; arch 05 lines 259 to 271; arch 11 lines 578, 580)

- **One link per person, read through shares.** Each person links their own calendar (S1-24; S2-06), Google or Microsoft, busy times only (MC 8); Apple iCloud is not linked (Q-11; `calendar_links_provider_known`). One person has one calendar across every company (S1-24; FS line 743; 02-contracts row K-27: "calendar links per person"; K-16 v1 `calendarLinkId`: "a link belongs to a person, never to one business"), so a person has one live link (`calendar_links_one_live_per_person`), with one change subscription, one Google channel or one Graph subscription (K-16 v1 default 16: Graph's 409 answers the person's one subscription), and one set of busy blocks. Every Tool API read is scoped to one business (arch 10 line 546), so each business the person works in reads the link through its own `calendar_link_shares` row and sees only free or busy through it: the link's state and its blocks' times, never the tokens or what an event is (S11-43: "Each business sees only busy times from the other"). S1-38's carry-over ("The ID, mobile, calendar and bill card carry over, never asked twice") adds the next business's share in the person's own call; nothing is connected twice (default 6). It is connected on a page the person already has open (registration screen 16, the owner console, the staff page); no link is texted for it (Q-10).
- **Connecting, then linked** (K-16 v1 `connectStart`, `connectReturn`, `tokensKept`). The connect's start writes the link row first (K-16: "The caller (C-189) writes the link row first, new or the dropped one being reconnected"): a new row is `connecting`, with no tokens and no `linked_at`, written on the runtime side by K-05's commit with the business's share beside it; a dropped link being reconnected stays `dropped` until its return. A connecting link is never read or watched (`calendar_links_connecting_unread`) and takes no time out of offer (S1-24's safe default: "nothing blocked yet"); a start not finished, declined or with scopes missing leaves it connecting, and the next start reuses it (default 13). The connect's return is the calendar adapter's write on the runtime side, since only it holds the tokens and only the runtime side reaches `sealing_keys` (migration 0003): it seals the tokens under the person's key and moves the row to `linked` with `linked_at` (`calendar_links_tokens_while_live`, `calendar_links_linked_has_time`; `refuse-connecting-with-tokens`), then the watch and the first read follow.
- **Tokens.** The provider's OAuth tokens are one value sealed under the person's key in K-04 `sealing_keys` (`sealedBy` `person_id`; arch 05 line 271), never plain and never held by reference to a key row (rule 6). Only the runtime side writes or reads them: the adapter seals them at the return, unseals them for every call (reads, the fresh free/busy read, the events out) and seals each refresh again (K-16 v1 `tokensKept`). The Tool API can neither read nor write them (rule 5). A connecting or unlinked row has none.
- **Sync marks.** `sync_cursor` (Google's sync token or Microsoft's delta link; null forces a full read), `synced_at`, and the change subscription (`subscription_id`, Google's `subscription_resource_id`, `subscribed_at`, `subscription_expires_at`), renewed when half its life is left (arch 05 line 271), written from K-16 v1's `watch` and `renew` answers; `subscription_resource_id`'s writer is K-16 v1.1's watch and renew answers (lead card T-1082), since v1's do not carry it. Rosie reads 90 days ahead, repeating events expanded, and never offers a linked person's time past 90 days after `synced_at` (arch 05 line 265). A lost push is covered by the fresh free/busy read at save time and the 30-minute poll (arch 11 line 578). One version covers the tokens and the sync marks (rule 3).
- **Dropped.** A renewal still failing after the effects retries (arch 05 line 271; arch 10 line 542), or tokens the provider refuses, drop the link (`dropped_at`, `dropped_why`): the owner of each business that reads it is texted at once; that person's open times come off at each of them; booked visits stay; the rest of the team is still offered; not reconnected in 24 hours, one reminder (`drop_reminded_at`) (default 7). The reconnect's return moves the row back to `linked` with the new tokens and `linked_at`, its drop marks cleared and its cursor and `synced_at` null (the new `linked_at` is past the old `synced_at`: `calendar_links_times_in_order`), so everything since is read and any clash goes to the owner first, after the clash check's fresh read (arch 11 line 580; S12-13, S12-14; K-09 v1 `calendarLinkBack`).
- **Unlinked.** The person takes it off, leaves every team that reads it (RULES 8.7) or links another calendar (default 14): `unlinked`, its tokens and subscription cleared in the same write, its shares ended and its busy blocks deleted. A business that stops reading it ends only its own share; the commit that ends a link's last live share unlinks the link (default 15).
- **Busy blocks** hold start, end, source and the event's zone, never a title, description, place, attendee or organizer: the table has no column for one (`refuse-busy-block-title`). Every event counts as busy, tentative ones too, except one marked free or a declined invite; such events are never written (arch 05 line 265). A calendar block is its link's, written once by the runtime side's sync with no business, and each business reads it through its live share. A clash of a new busy block with a booked visit goes to the owner at once and the visit stays (S12-33; arch 11 line 578), with the title never shown. A photo block (S1-24: "Or Sam sends a photo of the schedule") is written only in the commit of the owner's yes to the read-back of its times (`busy_blocks_source_fields`; arch 05 line 263; limit 6), for the person whose schedule it is, at that owner's business.

## Times and zones

- **Every time of day names its zone in its row.** `weekly_hours` and `classes` hold local times (`time`) with `time_zone`, the IANA zone they are read in.
- **Every instant is `timestamptz`** (UTC), and every window (`time_off.during`, `class_sessions.during`, `busy_blocks.during`) is a `tstzrange` `[start, end)` with the zone it is read back in named in the row. A whole day runs from midnight to midnight in that zone; "8:00am the day before" (S11-44) is read in it too.
- **Which zone:** the visit's zone, else the business's (CQ-05; K-04 default 9; K-25 rule 8). For a business's own hours, windows and classes that is the business's zone (K-04 `businesses.time_zone`), copied into the row when it is written, so a location in another zone (S11-53: "A second place, or a new state") keeps its own. A busy block keeps the event's zone as the provider gives it, else the calendar's (arch 05 line 265: "Times are kept in UTC with the event's zone"); a photo block, the business's.

## Database rules beyond the table list

The table list's meta-schema has no place for these; the migrations must produce them too, and T-104 checks them.

1. **Same schema as core:** the seven tables live beside K-04's, K-22's, K-23's and K-25's, and their foreign keys name K-04's, K-23's and K-25's tables by key (`businesses.id`, `persons.id`, `resources (id, kind)`, `locations.id`, `places_or_things.id`, `services (id, business_id)`) with no schema prefix. Nothing here refers to a K-22 table.
2. **Extensions:** `btree_gist` (K-04 v1 rule 1), for EXCLUDE's `=` on `text` and `smallint` beside `&&` on ranges. `weekly_hours_no_overlap` compares spans as `tsrange(date '2000-01-03' + opens_at, date '2000-01-03' + closes_at, '[)')`, an immutable expression on one fixed day, so `24:00` ends the day. No PostGIS type here.
3. **Versions:** all seven tables have `version integer not null default 1`, raised by exactly one by every update through K-04's `enforce_version_raise()` (migration 0002) as a `BEFORE UPDATE` trigger named `<table>_version_raised` (23514 otherwise), and compared and set by each writer (arch 01 line 85; arch 10 line 506: "Versions and compare-and-set on ... schedules"). The runtime side's sync writes raise it too, so two writers of one link never both move it. One version covers a link's tokens and its sync marks: a token refresh during a call (K-16 v1 `tokensKept`) raises it, so a caller whose compare-and-set of the cursor or the subscription then fails with `versionChanged` re-reads the row and applies its write again, which is safe (the same cursor reads the same changes: K-16 v1 `readChanges`).
4. **Ids** are `text`, opaque strings code makes (CQ-09). The provider's strings (`sync_cursor`, `subscription_id`, `subscription_resource_id`, `provider_event_ref`) are kept as given and never parsed.
5. **Row-level security, and the Tool API's grants** (arch 10 line 546; K-04 v1 rule 4; K-22 v1 rule 4's `rosie.person_id`, the person a call acts for, read as `nullif(current_setting('rosie.person_id', true), '')`): `weekly_hours`, `time_off`, `classes`, `class_sessions` and `calendar_link_shares` are scoped by `business_id`, so a Tool API call sees and writes only rows whose `business_id` equals its `rosie.business_id`. `calendar_links` and `busy_blocks` are scoped `none` in the table list, because a link and a calendar block are a person's, across every business (FS line 743): a business reads them only through its own live share, by the policies below. A photo block has its business, and the commit writes it per write under that business (the Tool API side, K-05 v1 "Scope"), where the policies hold it there; a link is a table scoped none with no business, so K-05's commit writes it on the runtime side. The Tool API role holds:
   - `weekly_hours`: `SELECT`, `INSERT`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE`.
   - `time_off`, `classes`, `class_sessions`: `SELECT`, `INSERT`, `UPDATE`; no `DELETE` (42501): a window is cancelled, a class set inactive, a session cancelled.
   - `calendar_links`: `SELECT` on every column but `tokens`, of the rows its select policy admits: `exists (select 1 from calendar_link_shares s where s.calendar_link_id = calendar_links.id and s.business_id = current_setting('rosie.business_id', true) and s.ended_at is null) or person_id = nullif(current_setting('rosie.person_id', true), '')`, a link its business reads through a live share, or the call's own person's link (as S1-38's carry-over finds it). So a read naming `tokens`, a search by it or `select *` is refused (42501; `refuse-calendar-tokens-read`), and a business that reads no share of a link gets no row. No `INSERT`, `UPDATE` or `DELETE` (42501): every write of a link is the runtime side's (below), so no Tool API call writes, clears or replaces tokens, and none writes a link for anyone.
   - `calendar_link_shares`: `SELECT` of its business's rows; `INSERT` of `id`, `calendar_link_id`, `person_id`, `business_id` and `shared_at` only, under the insert policy `with check (business_id = current_setting('rosie.business_id', true) and person_id = nullif(current_setting('rosie.person_id', true), ''))`, so a business reads a calendar only when the calendar's own person shares it, in that person's own call at that business (S1-24: "Each person can link their own calendar"; the connect's start, S1-38's carry-over, a team member joining another business: FS line 743); a share for someone else's calendar, for another business or from a call with no person is refused (42501; `refuse-share-for-another-person`), and `calendar_link_shares_link_fk` holds the share's person to the link's. `ended_at` and `version` are not granted on insert, so a share starts live at version 1. `UPDATE` of `ended_at` and `version` only, under the update policy `using (business_id = current_setting('rosie.business_id', true) and ended_at is null) with check (business_id = current_setting('rosie.business_id', true) and ended_at is not null)`: a business only ends its own live share (the person takes it off this business; the owner removes them from the team: RULES 8.7) and never reopens one. No `DELETE`.
   - `busy_blocks`: `SELECT` of the rows its select policy admits: `(source = 'photo' and business_id = current_setting('rosie.business_id', true)) or (source = 'calendar' and exists (select 1 from calendar_link_shares s where s.calendar_link_id = busy_blocks.calendar_link_id and s.business_id = current_setting('rosie.business_id', true) and s.ended_at is null))`, its own photo blocks and the calendar blocks of the links it reads (`refuse-busy-block-other-business-read`); `INSERT`, `UPDATE` and `DELETE` only of its own photo rows, by the policies `with check (business_id = current_setting('rosie.business_id', true) and source = 'photo')` for insert and update and `using (business_id = current_setting('rosie.business_id', true) and source = 'photo')` for update and delete: a calendar block written by the Tool API is refused (42501), and its update or delete changes no row.
   - The runtime side is unscoped, as K-04 rule 4 allows for jobs that work across businesses, and reads and writes every column, the tokens included: the connect's start (C-189 through K-05's commit: the connecting row; the share goes under the business, above), the connect's return (K-16 v1 `connectReturn`: the adapter seals the tokens and writes them with state `linked` and `linked_at`; for a dropped link reconnected, it also clears the drop marks and nulls `sync_cursor` and `synced_at`), the watch and renewals (K-16 v1 `watch`, `renew`: the subscription; `subscription_resource_id` once K-16 v1.1's answers carry it, lead card T-1082), calendar sync in and the 30-minute poll (C-136, C-137, K-16 v1 `readChanges`: busy blocks, `sync_cursor`, `synced_at`), the fresh free/busy read (K-16 v1 `freeBusy`: C-133's booking write and the clash check after K-09 v1's `calendarLinkBack`) and the calendar effects (C-138, C-109: K-16 v1 `calendarWrite`, `calendarUpdate`, `calendarDelete`), which unseal the tokens and seal each refresh again, a drop and its reminder (C-182), an unlink (the person's own, through K-05's commit; the last share ended; another calendar linked) and erasure.
6. **Sealed field:** `calendar_links.tokens`, sealed under the key of the person in `person_id` (K-04 `sealing_keys`; migration 0003's seal, unseal and erase, reached only from the runtime side; arch 01 line 93). The wrapping key is a Workers secret, named and never stored (rule 7). Erasing the person's key (RULES 4.10) leaves the tokens unreadable, and the erasure job unlinks the person's link and ends its shares.
7. **Rules across rows and tables, kept by code** (not constraints): a row's location, resource, space, class and teacher are its business's (K-04 `resources` and K-23 `locations` and `places_or_things` have no key with the business); a worker's spans and time off name a worker resource of a person with a role at the business; a session's reservations hold its `during`; its K-04 resource is set inactive and its reservations released when it is cancelled, in the same commit; its `class_seats.capacity` starts from its class's seats, and its service is its class's; a share's person and a photo block's person have a role at its business; a link's live shares end when it is unlinked, in the same commit, and the commit that ends a link's last live share unlinks it; a time off `asked` lapses at 8:00am the day before in its zone (a K-04 clock row); a busy block is written only for an event that counts as busy, never for Rosie's own event (K-07 `effects.provider_object_id`), and only for the next 90 days; a connecting link takes no time out of offer; a dropped link's person is not offered at any business that reads it.

7. **A drop is the link's:** a dropped link takes the person's open times out at every business that reads it; the runtime side finds its live shares and tells each business (K-09 v1 `calendarLinkDropped` under each business, so each owner is texted: S12-13), and the one reminder after 24 hours goes the same way (S12-14).
8. **Busy blocks are deleted once they end:** no source keeps a past busy time, and nothing reads one (the clash check looks at visits ahead).
9. **Time off's statuses** (`asked`, `ok`, `declined`, `lapsed`, `cancelled`) from S11-43 to S11-45 and S11-89; `asked` and `ok` take times out; the owner's own block is `ok` at once; who may answer is the owner, or an admin with the power (K-22 `team_powers`).
10. **A session is made in one commit** with its K-04 `resources` row (kind `class_session`) and `class_seats` counter, whose capacity starts from the class's seats; a one-off session has no class.
11. **The tokens are one sealed value** (the refresh token, and the access token with its expiry), whose inner form is K-16's.
12. **A photo block's person** is the person whose schedule it is, the owner who sent it by default (S1-24 reads Sam's own schedule).
13. **A link starts connecting:** the connect's start writes the row with no tokens and no linked time, so nothing is read, watched or blocked (S1-24's safe default: "nothing blocked yet"); a start not finished, declined or with scopes missing leaves it connecting for the next start to reuse; the return seals the tokens and moves it to linked (K-16 v1 `connectStart`, `connectReturn`). A reconnect leaves a dropped link dropped until its return. No source names the state.
14. **Another calendar:** linking another calendar unlinks the old link first (its tokens, subscription and blocks go, its shares end), then a new row starts connecting, so a person never has two live links.
15. **The last share:** the commit that ends a link's last live share unlinks the link, since no business reads it any more (RULES 4.10: nothing kept that is no longer needed).
