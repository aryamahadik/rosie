### Which part owns what

One fact lives in one place; this part names other parts' rows by their keys and copies a value only where the sources say the booking keeps it as it was made.

- **K-04 core** (frozen) owns the booking head (`bookings`: id, business, flow, stage entry, booked, checked_in, done or cancelled, booker, person served, payer, private, deceased, version), reservations (the booked time itself, `reservations.during`, with EXCLUDE per worker, room or vehicle), persons, handles, phones, open questions, turns, the event log and the sealing keys. T-186 builds the head as migration 0007. Anything this part would need on the head is an open gap for a K-04 minor version (below), never a column here.
- **K-25 services and prices** (frozen) owns services, ways (`service_money`) and price rules. A line names the service, the way and the rule it copied (composite keys hold them to one service and one business) and copies the way's path, the service's price kind and unit, the price, the deposit and the fee: K-25 updates a way in place and closes a rule at a new date, and the booking keeps what it was made with (RULES 3.7; S11-54, S11-56).
- **K-26 rules** (frozen) owns the owner's rules and price OK limits. Its rows are never edited in place or deleted (an update only closes one), so the terms snapshot names the rows in force (`booking_terms_rules`) instead of copying their values; a quote names the limit that asked for an OK.
- **K-28 customers** (frozen) owns the customer's record at the business: the name, the contact on file and the way they are reached (`customers.way`, spelt as K-01's `bookingValues.reach`). The booking's reach is that way of whoever it reaches (default 8). A link names the K-04 handle it went to, never a copy of the number.
- **K-29 conversations and consent** (frozen) owns every yes: the terms the customer ticked are its `consents` row of kind `ticked_terms` naming the booking (its README: "the same words are K-30's terms_text"), which the snapshot names by id; the yes to waitlist messages is a `texts` row; the chat links are its `chat_links`, whose first `issued_at` is copied into `first_link_given_at` (Q-33).
- **K-23 business setup** (frozen) owns the locations and travel areas an address is checked against, and the default free-cancel time per flow (H06), which the snapshot copies when the way has none. **K-22 people and access** (frozen) owns staff and who may OK what; the flow B seed's pro (Jordan) is its staff member.
- **K-08 event and trace** (frozen): `owner_oks` holds the answer's five ownerYes fields as columns, and `booking_changes.approved_by` holds K-08's `approval` JSON as K-04's `event_log.approved_by` does.
- **K-31 the visit** (F17) will name `booking_visits.id` for check-ins, DONE and no-shows and owns FS line 729's `checked_in_at`, `done_at` and `done_by`; **K-32 money** (F16) will name `booking_lines.id` for payments, fees, refunds and credits (FS line 739: `credit.booking_service_id`).

### The booking values (K-01 v1 bookingValues; SPEC-master 5e)

`made_by`, `approval`, `timing`, `times` and `taken` are the choice groups the path model reads (K-01 "a group left out has its first value"). Each is null until decided and then set once (FS line 752; A4-98: "Decided once ... never decided again"). The others live where they belong: `privacy` is K-04's `private` (default 9), `reach` is K-28's way (default 8), `paidby` is per line (`booking_lines.paid_by`), and the event groups (charge, contact, payer) are read from K-32's payments, K-28 and K-29's contact rows, and the head's payer.

### Times (rule 9)

The booked time is K-04's `reservations.during`. The arrival window (B4-15: "arriving 8–10am") or a sent-now estimate (B4-91) is the visit's `arrival_window`, inside the reserved time that also holds the job's length (`seed-booking-flow-b`: reserved 8 to 11, arriving 8 to 10). The free-cancel time is worked out when the booking is written from the cancel rule the snapshot copies; a move before it moves it with the visit (A6-12), and a late move the owner accepts keeps it (A7-03). Every instant is UTC and is shown in the visit's zone: `booking_addresses.zone` of position 1 for a visit at the customer's place, else the business's `time_zone` (S13-03; CQ-05's default; K-04 v1 default 9; K-25 v1 rule 8). The one clock time kept here, a copied day-before cancel time, names the zone it was read in (`booking_terms.cutoff_zone`).

### Money (rule 9)

Every amount is integer minor units in `bigint` (cents for USD) with its three-letter currency, never a decimal (CQ-09; K-25 v1 rule 7; K-33 v1 rule 5). A line copies the price shown before the yes: on a list path the resolved rule's price times the units, on a photos path the picked option's price, on a visit path the promised visit price (A4-13, B4-14) and any 'from' price; a deposit is worked out in minor units when copied (a percent of the price rounds as default 13 says); the booking's one fee is the largest of its open lines', never more than what was paid now (S13-55), which K-32 applies.

## Database rules beyond the table list

The table list's meta-schema has no place for these; the migrations must produce them too, and T-107 checks them.

1. **Same schema as core:** the 13 tables live beside K-04's and the other parts' tables, and their foreign keys name them by key with no schema prefix (`bookings.id`, `businesses.id`, `persons.id`, `person_handles (person_id, handle_id)`, `reservations.id`, `resources.id`, `turns.id`, `open_questions.id`, `services (id, business_id)`, `service_money (id, service_id)`, `price_rules (id, service_id)`, `locations.id`, `rules.id`, `price_ok_limits.id`, `consents.id`). The migration creates the tables before their foreign keys (`booking_changes` before `booking_lines`, `quote_options` before `booking_lines`).
2. **Extensions:** none beyond K-04's (`btree_gist`); `tstzrange` is built in, and no PostGIS type is used here.
3. **Ids** are `text`, opaque strings code makes (CQ-09); `booking_details.number` is a code-made short string, unique within the business, never parsed.
4. **Versions:** the tables marked versioned have `version integer not null default 1`, raised by every update and compared and set by a commit (arch 01 line 85; K-04 v1 rule 2). `booking_terms`, `booking_terms_rules`, `booking_changes` and `page_answers` are only inserted and carry none.
5. **Row-level security per business** (arch 10 line 546; K-04 v1 rule 4): every table is scoped by `business_id`, read and written by a Tool API call only in its own business (`business_id = nullif(current_setting('rosie.business_id', true), '')`). K-04's head has no key on `(id, business_id)`, so a foreign key cannot hold a row's business to its booking's; instead, on every table with `booking_id`, the Tool API's insert and update policy also requires `exists (select 1 from bookings b where b.id = <table>.booking_id and b.business_id = <table>.business_id)`, which K-04's own row-level security limits to the call's scope: a call can never attach a row to another business's booking (`refuse-line-on-other-business-booking`, 42501). The runtime side (unscoped, as K-04 rule 4 allows) holds the same by code. `quote_options` and the keys to K-25 hold their business by composite foreign keys. The runtime side reads unscoped only for: finding a link by its code's hash (the page server, before any business is known), the dispatcher reading a booking for a message, clock firings (deadlines, reminders), and the 45-day erasure.
6. **What may be written, by privilege and one trigger** (both application roles, the Tool API and the runtime side): no `DELETE` on any of the 13 tables (42501). Insert only, no `UPDATE`: `booking_terms`, `booking_terms_rules`, `booking_changes`, `page_answers` (42501: `refuse-terms-snapshot-update`, `refuse-change-row-update`). `UPDATE` of named columns only: `booking_lines` (`state`, `removed_at`, `removed_by_change_id`, `version`: the copy is never changed, `refuse-booking-line-price-change`, 42501), `booking_visits` (`reservation_id`, `arrival_window`, `cutoff_at`, `version`: the code never changes), `secure_links` (`ended_at`, `ended_why`, `version`), `waitlist_entries` (`ended_at`, `ended_why`, `version`). `secure_links.code_sha256` is outside the Tool API's `SELECT` grant (a whole-row read is refused, `refuse-link-code-read`, 42501); the runtime side reads it. `booking_details` takes updates, but a `BEFORE UPDATE` trigger `booking_details_set_once` refuses any change to `booking_id`, `business_id`, `number`, `door` or `made_by`, and any change to `approval`, `timing`, `times`, `taken`, `first_link_given_at`, `staff_told_at`, `request_ended_at` or `request_ended_why` once set, raising SQLSTATE 23514 with constraint `booking_details_set_once` (`refuse-booking-value-reset`); a null value may be set once. The function body is the one T-039's check used: `if <a listed column changed as above> then raise exception 'a booking value is set once and never changed (FS line 752)' using errcode = 'check_violation', constraint = 'booking_details_set_once'; end if; return new;`.
7. **Sealed** (arch 01 line 93; K-04 v1 rule 6): `booking_visits.check_in_code` under the key of `code_for_person_id`, `booking_addresses.address` under `sealed_for_person_id`, and `page_answers.answer` under `person_id`, each in K-04's `sealing_keys`. Only the hash of a link's code is kept (BP rule 7). Words a person ticked are K-29's, never sealed (consent proofs survive).
8. **Retention:** a booking and the words agreed are kept 7 years (A6-10's rule; S13-08; FS line 754); no application role deletes a row (rule 6). A person's delete within 45 days (RULES 4.10) deletes their sealing key (C-156), so their sealed codes, addresses and answers can never be read again while the rows and their plain fields stay; plain fields here hold no words a person typed. The purge after 7 years is a job with no contract yet (open gaps).
9. **Times and money** as the sections above say: instants in `timestamptz` and ranges in `tstzrange` (UTC), shown in the visit's zone, else the business's; `need_by` is a day in that zone; money in `bigint` minor units with a currency.
10. **Rules across rows and tables, kept by code** (not constraints): a details row is written with its head; a row's booking is its business's on the runtime side (rule 5); a line's path is its way's at copy time and one of the master's 28 for the booking's flow (K-25's own list); a line's price is the resolver's rule price times units (list), the picked option's (photos) or the promised visit price (visit), shown to the customer before the yes; a visit's reservation is one of its booking's (K-04 `reservations.booking_id`); `first_link_given_at` is the earliest K-29 chat link issued for the booking; a snapshot names exactly the K-26 rows in force at `written_at` for the booking's flow and kind of work, and copies the way's own cancel rule, else K-23's default; the terms consent is of kind `ticked_terms` for the same booking; an owner's answer comes from the owner or an admin the owner lets answer (K-22); approval follows K-26's OK rules; a sent quote's options never change; a change's `booking_version_before` is the head's version it read; the private fields K-04 keeps out of `event_log` stay out of `booking_changes.old_values` and `new_values`.

## Open gaps

- **Arch 09's count:** 12 tables for "Bookings and changes", with no names; this part holds 13 beside K-04's head and reservations, so the count cannot be matched table for table (as K-28, K-29, K-33 and K-37 found for theirs).
- **No `(id, business_id)` key on K-04's head:** a row's business is held to its booking's by the Tool API's insert and update policy (rule 5) and by code on the runtime side, not by a foreign key; a K-04 minor version adding `bookings_id_business_key` would let a K-30 minor version add composite keys. Proposed for QUESTIONS.md.
- **No stage for a request that ended unbooked:** K-04's stages are entry, booked, checked_in, done and cancelled; v1 uses `cancelled` with `request_ended_*` saying it was never booked (`seed-terms-snapshot`'s waitlist request). A K-04 minor version may add a stage. Proposed for QUESTIONS.md.
- **The arrival window's length** (B4-15: "Arrival windows are 2 hours, as Sam set at sign-up") has no K-23 column (K-23 `flow_defaults.arrive` holds only setstart or window); the visit holds the window's instants, and the setting itself is a K-23 gap.
- **A visit with no K-04 resource:** K-04 has no resource kind for a K-23 place or thing (a table, a suite), so such a visit's `reservation_id` is null until a K-04 minor version adds one; the time of a booking with nothing reserved is then not held here (the lead's rule: the booked time is K-04's).
- **Which line belongs to which visit** (a first step paired with the visit: A4-14, A7-05) is not stored: every open line applies to every visit of the booking in v1.
- **Drive time between flow B jobs** needs each job's map point, and the address is sealed: code geocodes the unsealed address through K-17 when it needs a point, and no point is kept. A sealed or coarse point is a v1.1 column if search needs it.
- **Notices:** no source gives a notice's words (pf.rules: "from the state form"); `notices_shown` keeps the words as shown, and no seed holds one.
- **The terms version's form:** no source names a scheme; code names the checked terms text it showed.
- **K-08 event kinds** for these rows (a request opened, a booking value set, a line copied or removed, a snapshot written, a quote priced or sent, an owner's ask and answer, a change, a link issued or ended, an answer saved, a waitlist entry): K-08 v1.1 has `booking.created`, `stage_changed`, `flagged` and `note_sealed` only. A K-08 minor version before C-099 commits them (the CQ-45 and CQ-56 pattern).
- **K-05 calls** that read and write these rows (a booking's record, a line's copy, a quote and its OK, a change, a link by its hash, a page answer) come with K-05's minor versions and K-09 v1.1 to v1.5; writes with no turn wait for CQ-44's commit.
- **An organization that books** (A4-89) and **a booker's authority papers** (A4-64): no organization entity (K-28's gap); the papers are a page answer of kind `authority`.
- **A plan check** (A4-37, planbill) and what a plan pays: K-32's, or a K-30 minor version.
- **A sent quote's options** are held unchanged by code, not a constraint (the owner's change happens before sending).
- **The 7-year purge** has no contract yet; C-156 handles the 45-day erasure by deleting keys.
- **A customer's own zone** ("A booker in another time zone sees the place's time and their own", A4-14): no person carries a zone (K-04 v1 default 9).
- **CQ-09's id forms:** ids and booking numbers are opaque text until the CTO rules.
