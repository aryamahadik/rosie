### Who owns what

One fact lives in one place. Where another part already holds a field, this part refers to it and never repeats it.

- **K-04 core** owns businesses, persons and resources: a worker is a `resources` row of kind `worker` with its person, which a price rule names (`price_rules_worker_fk`, through K-04's `resources_id_kind_key`). Nothing here refers to K-22, which is not frozen.
- **K-22 people and access** (in review, T-031) owns the staff details, skills (T04: who takes which work) and who may OK what. **The price by person (T06) is here**, as a price rule with a worker (02-contracts section 8, key `team`: "K-25: staff prices (FS T06)"); a K-22 column holding it would be the same fact twice.
- **K-23 business setup** owns the business's default free-cancel time per flow (`flow_defaults`, H06), its locations with their travel areas (L02, L03) and its places and things (K04). A way names its location; a way's own free-cancel time, where the owner set one, wins over K-23's default, and with none set K-23's applies. The travel fee for farther towns (L03) stays K-23's. K-23's README calls this part's link from spaces to services `services.places`: it is `item_services` here, one row per link, so each carries a foreign key.
- **K-24 kinds of work** owns the kinds (`services.kind_id` refers to `kinds.id`) and each trade's money paths per flow (`kind_flows.combos`), the only paths offered on the money card (S1-13); a way's path is checked against them by code. K-24 refers to nothing here.
- **K-26 rules** owns the business's tip-line rule (kind `tip_line`), deposit and fee caps, the minimum job, bank transfer and the price OK limit (`price_ok_limits`). A way's own tip line, where set, wins over K-26's rule (K-26 v1 README: "K-25 services and prices owns each service's late-cancel and no-show fee (V08), a service path's tip setting (FS line 725) and price rules by worker, place and time"). K-26's rules for some services (QUESTIONS.md CQ-51) wait for a K-26 v1.1 column naming `services.id`, which is stable for it: a service is never deleted or re-keyed.
- **K-33 platform billing**: its money form is kept (whole cents in `bigint` with a three-letter currency code, K-33 v1 rule 5); Rosfield's own fee is never a price here.
- **K-30 bookings** (F15) copies a booking line's way and price in when it is made (FS lines 730, 751: path, price kind, price, before, after, deposit, fee), written once, so a change here applies to new bookings only (RULES 3.7). **K-32 money** (F16) names a service for packs and memberships (FS lines 737, 738).
- **K-02 v1.1** (photo reading, frozen with it, F14) reads a price list into these columns, named as S1-12's form names them ("Service, Price kind, Price, Time it takes"): `services.name`, `services.price_kind` (and `unit` for a per-unit line), `services.length`, and the service's own price rule's `price_cents` with `currency`.

### Where FS section 9's fields live

- FS `service` (line 724): `id`, `business_id`, `name`, `price_kind`, `length`, `active` and `extras` here; `order` is `position`; `places` are the service's `service_money` rows; `price` is the service's price rule with no conditions (default 3).
- FS `service_money` (line 725): `service_id`, `flow`, `place` (with `location_id`), `path`; of the amounts, `deposit` is `deposit_cents` or `deposit_percent` and `fee` is `fee_cents`, `price` is a price rule with that place, and `rest` and `paid` are worked out by code (the price less the deposit; on a full path, the price), never stored; `cutoff` is `cutoff_before` or `cutoff_day_before_at`; `tip on or off` is `tip_line`.
- FS section 2b `item_service` and FS line 741 `place_or_thing.services`: `item_services`. FS line 723 `staff.price` (T06): a price rule with a worker.

### Services (S1-12)

- **Price kind** is S1-12's four, K-02 v1.1's field: `one_price`, `per_hour`, `per_unit` (with its `unit`, such as night), `by_answers` (set by the customer's answers). When the price is known, from the list, from photos first or at the visit (FS row V02, which also says "price kind"), is the path's first part on each way, so one service may be listed at the shop and quoted at a home.
- **Length** is the time it takes (V04), which a booking holds: per hour, the shortest booking (event hours' 1-hour minimum); per unit or by answers, the time one unit takes (about 35 minutes a person), so a booking of several holds that many.
- **Extras** is FS's Service.extras: the profession file's `pf.services` entry and `pf.questions` for this service, whole, as JSON text the AI reads and Settings shows. No code reads a sub-key, and no price or length comes from it (BP rule 4: every price, amount and length is a typed column code fills).
- **A free service** is a list price of 0 on a nothing-before, nothing-after path (FS row V02; S1-12: "A service the list calls free is set as free: nothing taken, nothing after").

### Ways (S1-13, S1-43)

- **One way per service and place** ("A service in two places has two prices and two money rows"; the forms: "picks ONE path per service and place"): `business` at a shop or office, `customer` at the customer's place (flow B), `meeting_spot`, or `video` (video or phone, flow A). Every place but video names its location; at the customer's place it is the shop or office whose travel area (L02) the address is checked against (B4-77).
- **The path** is one of the master's 28 for the way's flow (D66: no hold; no deposit with nothing after; a visit price never taken in full). A deposit is a set amount or a whole percent (S1-13: "never more than the price known then"); the fee is the way's late-cancel and no-show fee, none on a nothing-taken path (FS section 4: "no fee possible"), and with a deposit and no fee, the deposit is the fee (S13-55). A booking with several services takes the largest fee, never more than what was paid now (RULES 3.3), in code.
- **A change applies to new bookings at once** (S11-56: "It's for new bookings only"): a way is updated in place, its version raised, and each booking keeps the way it copied (K-30). A place no longer offered is set inactive, never deleted.

### Prices (arch 05 line 253; arch 09 line 488)

- **Every price is a rule**: a service's own price has no condition; a price at one place names that way (`service_money_id`, such as the haircut at the customer's home, $75); a price by person names the worker (T06: "the same price whoever goes" unless the owner says, such as event hours, $140 with Andre); a price by time names days of the week and clock times in the business's zone (such as $40 after 5pm).
- **Effective dates price visits**: a rule prices visits that start in `[effective_from, effective_to)` (S11-54: "Sam raises the haircut to $40 for visits from Mon Nov 2. AI assistants show the new price for those dates at once"). A change closes one row and opens the next at the same instant: the $35 row gets `effective_to` and the $40 row starts then. A booking copies the price when it is made, so "Haircuts already booked keep $35, even after Nov 2" (RULES 3.7). `price_rules_one_in_effect` refuses two rows of one service with the same conditions in effect at once (23P01).
- **The price kind of the path** says what the price is: a set price on a list path; on a visit path, the visit's own price that is promised (A4-13, A4-94: the $25 consult, the $89 service call) and any `from_cents`, shown only as "from" (A4-05); a photos path takes the quote made for the booking (A4-08 to A4-12, K-30), never a rule.

**How a price is found** (C-132, the price resolver; search's five rows show the same, arch 05 line 239):

1. Take the service's rules in effect at the visit's start.
2. Keep those whose every condition holds: its way is the booking's, its worker gives the work, its days and clock times hold the visit's start in the business's zone.
3. The rule with the most conditions set wins (place, worker, time: 0 to 3).
4. Between rules with as many: the winner the owner named for their tie; with no answer yet, the lower price (S13-25 rule 19).
5. The booking line copies it, written once (K-30). A time being saved keeps the price on the customer's screen (S11-54), which the hold carries (K-30).

**A possible tie** is two rules of one service with as many conditions that can both apply to one booking (such as a place rule and a time rule). Code finds it when the owner saves the second, the read-back asks which wins (arch 05 line 253, DD), and `price_rule_ties` keeps the question (`asked_at`) and the answer (`winner_rule_id`, who and when). A rule's own row never changes to answer it.

### Spaces and things (S1-81; FS section 2b; Q-46)

`item_services` lists which services each space or thing takes ("Later: the services that use a space aren't bookable until it's listed"). Customers then pick the space and its size, never a person (A4-62). The item card's own fields (FS section 2b: price, money combo, deposit, fee and cutoff changed for one item; its status) come with the Items tab, built after journey 11's core pages (Q-46's default), as a minor version.

## Database rules beyond the table list

The table list's meta-schema has no place for these; the migrations must produce them too, and the contract test checks them.

1. **Same schema as core:** the five tables live beside K-04's, K-23's and K-24's, and their foreign keys name them by key (`businesses.id`, `persons.id`, `resources (id, kind)`, `locations.id`, `places_or_things.id`, `kinds.id`) with no schema prefix. Nothing here refers to K-22, K-26, K-27 or K-30.
2. **Extensions:** `btree_gist` (K-04 v1 rule 1), for EXCLUDE's `=` on text and time beside `&&` on `tstzrange(effective_from, effective_to, '[)')`; a null `effective_to` is an open end. No PostGIS type here.
3. **Versions:** `services`, `service_money`, `price_rules` and `price_rule_ties` are updated, so each has `version integer not null default 1`, raised by every update and compared and set by a commit (arch 01 line 85; K-04 v1 rule 2). `item_services` rows are only inserted and deleted, and carry none.
4. **Ids** are `text`, opaque strings code makes (CQ-09); `kind_id` is K-24's kind id.
5. **Row-level security per business** (arch 10 line 546; K-04 v1 rule 4): every table is scoped by `business_id`, which ways, prices, ties and links copy from their service and hold to it by foreign keys on `services_id_business_key`.
6. **Who may delete, by privilege:** the Tool API may select, insert and update `services`, `service_money`, `price_rules` and `price_rule_ties`, and never delete them (42501): a service or a way stops by `active` false, a price by `effective_to`, so ids stay stable (CQ-51) and history stays (arch 09 line 488). It may select, insert and delete `item_services` (S1-81 and Settings). A price rule's values are never edited in place: an update only closes it, which the commit keeps, as K-26 v1 does for rules.
7. **Money** is whole cents in `bigint` with a three-letter currency code (CQ-09; K-33 v1 rule 5's form), in any currency: the prices are the business's own. Code keeps one currency per business.
8. **Times:** `weekdays`, `from_time` and `until_time` are read in the business's zone (`businesses.time_zone`); effective dates are instants in UTC, matched against a visit's start.
9. **Rules across rows and tables, kept by code** (not constraints): a service's kind is one of the business's (K-23 `company_kinds`); a way's path is one its kind's plug-in offers in that flow (K-24 `kind_flows.combos`), and with no money through Rosie (K-23 `no_money`; fact `nomoney`) only nothing before and after `counter` (A) or `own` (B); a place S1-43 allows; a way's location is the business's, of the place's kind (a shop or office; a meeting spot; for the customer's place, one with a travel area); a card or full path takes a fee at setup, except sensitive work (FS row V08; RULES 3.5); a deposit is never more than the price known then; a list-priced way has a price rule in effect; a rule's worker and an item's space are the business's; the tie found at a save; the lower price until it is answered.
