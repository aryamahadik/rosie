### Who is told (arch 06 line 329)

- `hand_offs.kind` follows arch 06 line 329's rows: `crisis_words` (the owner and Rosie's team at once: `escalate_at = handed_at`, no clock row, `hand_offs_crisis_words_at_once`); `emergency` and `threat` (the owner; after 5 minutes with no reply or console tap, whoever they named and Rosie's team: `escalate_at = handed_at + 5 minutes`, `hand_offs_emergency_threat_five_minutes`); `other` (any other hand-off, S13-29 rules 53 and 54: the owner in text hours; unanswered for 2 days, Rosie's team: `escalate_at` at least 2 days after `handed_at`, since code counts the 2 days from when the owner is told in text hours, `hand_offs_other_two_days`).
- Harm to a child or older adult, and a report about the owner or staff, never go to the owner: they are K-34 `harm_reports` and page the team directly (`team_pages.reason` `harm_report`). A threat or emergency's safety read is K-34's `safety_outcomes` row (`safety_outcome_id`, required for the three safety kinds); the person's words stay sealed there, and `summary` holds only what the owner may hear (A3-25's "Urgent: a customer may need help"; A3-48's owner line).
- The answer: `answered_at`, `answered_via` (`reply` or `console_tap`) and `answered_by_person_id`, together. The escalation is a core `clock_rows` row (`escalation_clock_row_id`, cancelled when an answer comes first); when it fires, the team page is opened and `escalated_at` set.

### Pages and their acknowledgement

- `team_pages.reason` and the one pointer each needs (a CHECK per pointer): `hand_off` (`hand_off_id`; one page per hand-off), `owner_cannot_act` (word that the owner can't act or may have died: S11-19, S13-29 rule 57; `business_id`), `harm_report` (`harm_report_id`, K-34), `review_item` (`review_item_id`, K-37), `safety_timer_missed` (`safety_timer_id`, K-34: the team keeps trying until SAFE, S13-10), `passed_on` (`turn_id`: a reply that went out as "I've passed this on", arch 03 line 123), `hard_invariant_breach` (arch 08 line 447; `business_id` may be null).
- **Paged every 5 minutes until acknowledged** (arch 06 line 329, a DD): `repeat_minutes` is 5 for a repeating page, null for one paged once that waits in the team console. `times_paged` and `last_paged_at` count the pages; a clock row fires once (K-05 item 5), so each page arms a new core clock row and `next_page_clock_row_id` moves to it; acknowledging cancels it. An open repeating page always has its next clock row.
- **Who is paged:** every member of `team_page_recipients` whose `removed_at` is null, and for an emergency or a threat whoever the owner named (`named_person_id`, only on a `hand_off` page). Each page to each person is a core outbox row (send class safety), which K-07 and the dispatcher carry.
- **Acknowledged:** `state` `acknowledged` with `acknowledged_at`, `acknowledged_by_person_id` and `acknowledged_via` (`reply` or `console_tap`), together (`team_pages_acknowledged_together`), and no earlier than the last page.

### The recipient list (QUESTIONS.md M3-M3b.5)

- One row per member, live while `removed_at` is null (one live row per person). Each is reached in their own open chat with Rosie (`chat_handle_id`, whose chat state is core's `phones`), else by email (`email_handle_id`); at least one. Rosie never writes first (C9), so a member with no open chat gets pages by email until they text Rosie.
- Until launch the members are Jay and the CTO (Q-18). On staging, only roster test phones and the test console (BP 9.4): the staging database holds its own rows.

## Database rules beyond the table list

The table list's meta-schema has no place for these; the migrations must produce them too, and T-113 checks them.

1. **Same schema as core:** the three tables live beside K-04's tables, and their foreign keys name core tables by K-04's keys (`businesses.id`, `persons.id`, `handles.id`, `bookings.id`, `turns.id`, `clock_rows.id`, `outbox_rows.id`) and the K-34 and K-37 tables a page or hand-off points at by their keys (`safety_outcomes.id`, `harm_reports.id`, `safety_timers.id`, `review_items.id`), so the references carry no schema prefix. The migration therefore runs after C-170's (K-34) and C-173's (K-37).
2. **Who may do what, by privilege** (K-04 v1 rule 4's two sides): `hand_offs` is scoped by `business_id` like any business table, so the Tool API (the commit, and the owner's answer) reads and writes only its own business's rows. **`team_pages` is Rosie's team's:** the Tool API role may insert into it (the commit pages for crisis words, a harm report or a reply passed on) and has no `SELECT`, `UPDATE` or `DELETE` on it, so no business reads a page, its owner included (SQLSTATE 42501). **`team_page_recipients`** has no Tool API grant at all. The runtime side reads and writes both unscoped: the clock that escalates a hand-off or repeats a page, and the team console (C-194), which works on that side until K-21 names its role (K-37 v1 rule 2). A page acknowledged by a reply is written on the runtime side too (open gaps).
3. **Versions:** all three tables are updated (answered, escalated, paged again, acknowledged, a member removed), so each has `version integer not null default 1`, raised by every update and compared and set by a commit (arch 01 line 85; K-04 v1 rule 2).
4. **No private value in a plain column:** `hand_offs.summary` holds only what the owner may hear, and `team_pages.summary` ids, kinds and plain words. A person's words stay sealed in the K-34 row a hand-off or page points at (arch 09 line 492; RULES 4.5). This part seals nothing itself.
5. **Rules across tables, kept by code** (not constraints): a hand-off's `kind` matches its safety read's outcome (Crisis words, An emergency, A threat to staff or others); its alert outbox row (`alert_outbox_row_id`) is written in the same commit and linked by code; a page's `next_page_clock_row_id` names a live clock row; a page acknowledged by `reply` comes from one of the people paged; the members paged are those live on the list when each page goes.
6. **Ids** are `text`, opaque strings code makes (CQ-09).

## Open gaps

- **Arch 09's count:** 2 tables for "Notifications"; this part holds 3, the third being M3-M3b.5's recipient list. Arch 09 gives no names, so the count cannot be matched table for table (as K-29 and K-37 found for theirs).
- **Which pages repeat:** arch 06 line 329 says "paged every 5 minutes until acknowledged" only for an emergency or a threat after the owner's 5 minutes. Whether crisis words, a harm report, a missed timer, owner silence, a passed-on reply or a breach page repeat too is not in the sources; `repeat_minutes` may be 5 or null for each, and code chooses. Proposed for QUESTIONS.md with M3-M3b.5.
- **Whoever the owner named** (S13-29 rule 57): who that is lives in K-22 (not yet frozen; S2-27's on-call person is the nearest field). Here it is only the copied `told_person_id` / `named_person_id`; with none named, the team alone is paged.
- **The owner's late answer:** whether the owner's reply after the team was paged also acknowledges the page is not in the sources; v1 records it on the hand-off only.
- **A reply that acknowledges a page:** the Tool API role cannot update a page, so how a paged person's reply reaches the page (a K-05 or K-09 call on the runtime side) is K-05's and K-09's to state.
- **The text-hours start of the 2 days** (S11-19; arch 06 line 329 "the owner in text hours; unanswered for 2 days"): whether the 2 days count from the alert's delivery or from the start of the next text hours, and in which zone (CQ-05), is not in the sources; code computes `escalate_at`, never sooner than 2 days after `handed_at`.
- **K-07 and K-08 vocabularies:** the page sends' outbox states and the event kinds for a hand-off, a page and an acknowledgement are K-07's and K-08's.
- **K-37's review queue and pages:** K-37 v1's open gaps list "K-36's pages" as a possible review kind; here a page points at a review item instead, and K-37 needs no change.
- **Rosie's team as people:** K-04's `person_roles` has no team role (K-37 v1 open gaps); a member is a `persons` row named on `team_page_recipients`.
- **CQ-09's id forms:** ids are opaque text until the CTO rules.
